# THE PRICE OF THIRTY-EIGHT

Pain spikes through my chest—not the deep ache of muscle strain, but the clean-cut agony of cold steel driven into bone marrow. A reminder. Always a fucking reminder.

*If you disobey, it calcifies your heart.*

But thirty-eight orphans lived because I chose them instead.

The Fourth Law of the Weave says command has weight. Says sacrifice made at the crucible of necessity binds the living to the fallen. It doesn't say anything about how you're supposed to live with what that weight does to you.
