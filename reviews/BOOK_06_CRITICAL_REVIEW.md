## CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES

### MAJOR FLAWS:

**1. PACING DISTRIBUTION FAILURE**
The novel's energy concentrates in first third and final two chapters while middle section stalls. Chapters 6-10 need aggressive cutting—aim to remove 20% of words here to improve overall momentum.

**2. UNDERDEVELOPED ANTAGONIST**
Elias remains sketchy despite being central threat. His motivations unclear; his methods under-explained; his defeat lacks satisfying causation. Needed: stronger villainous presence through chapters 5-13 with clear objectives readers can track.

**3. EMOTIONAL STAKES BECOME ABSTRACT**
While Danny provides initial human stakes, the shift to "rescued children" as abstract group numbs emotional investment. Need 1-2 specific child characters who receive development—readers must care about individuals, not concepts.

**4. MAGIC SYSTEM UNDEREXPLOITED**
Telluric batteries have fascinating potential but remain underutilized. Mid-book could benefit from scene demonstrating harvesting process, creating visceral horror that justifies higher body count.
