### 2. CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES

#### **Flaw #1: Underdefined Antagonist**
The scrap-metal cartels never receive concrete characterization beyond generic criminality. Who leads them? What's their relationship to the telluric parasite—are they knowingly possessed, unwitting hosts, or something else? Without a properly humanized villain, Cole's vigilante actions feel directionless.

**Recommendation:** Add one scene showing cartel leadership—not necessarily sympathetic, but *specific*. Their motivations, their methods, their weaknesses.

#### **Flaw #2: Magic System Opacity**
While internally consistent, the mechanics remain opaque to readers. How do Apep nails work precisely? Why cold iron specifically? What are the rules of engagement with telluric entities?

**Recommendation:** Weave in brief exposition during downtime scenes. Bram could explain ward mechanics while working on equipment. Don't frontload it; reveal through character interaction.

#### **Flaw #3: Emotional Stakes Remain Abstract**
Cole survives various attacks, loads his shotgun, prepares for confrontation—but we never see what he fears losing. Is there someone back home? Does Local 405 mean more than just union dues?

**Recommendation:** Insert one vulnerable moment (Chapter 8 or 9) where Cole reflects on why this matters beyond pride or duty. This would deepen reader investment in his survival.
