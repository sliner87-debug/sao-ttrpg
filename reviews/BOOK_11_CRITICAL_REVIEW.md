### 2. CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES & SPECIFIC FLAWS

**Vulnerability #1: Structural Coherence**
The novel attempts dual timelines (pre-breach investigation + post-breach aftermath) but fails to establish clear temporal markers until Chapter 10. Readers may mistake early scenes as flashbacks rather than present-tense buildup.

**Vulnerability #2: Character Emotional Arc Incompleteness**
Mara's central trauma—the Sub-Station Seven fire that seals her "cynical devotion"—happens between chapters 12 and 15 with insufficient on-page development. We need to witness at least one scene of her *during* the event, not merely after, to earn emotional weight.

**Vulnerability #3: Worldbuilding Gap Regarding Aegis Doctrine**
Despite being "Aegis Commander," we learn almost nothing about this organization's actual function, hierarchy, or philosophy beyond vague references to "protection protocols." This creates frustrating opacity during crucial decision moments.

**Vulnerability #4: Unresolved Magic System Questions**
If telluric lines require cold-iron containment (established), why does Sub-Station Seven breach so catastrophically? The mechanics are hinted at but never adequately explained—readers may assume author doesn't know either.

**Vulnerability #5: Opening Scene Information Dump**
Chapter 1 dedicates first 8 paragraphs to procedural setup without establishing Mara's voice, personality, or stakes. This risks reader disengagement before worldbuilding pays narrative dividends.
