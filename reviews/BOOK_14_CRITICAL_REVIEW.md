## CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES & SPECIFIC FLAWS

### **Flaw #1: Magic System Opacity**
The greatest systemic failure is never explaining what the central conceit actually *means*. Telluric lines, Apep nails, blood-iron mechanics—they're furniture rather than functional magic. For readers unfamiliar with arcano-noir conventions (the target audience includes genre newcomers), this creates insurmountable comprehension barriers.

**Example Problem:** Chapter 3 describes Cole "pulling density from empty air," but never establishes rules for when/why/how this works. It reads as author convenience rather than character ability.

### **Flaw #2: Uneven POV Leverage**
Dual-POV structure wastes dramatic opportunity. When both narrators witness identical events (Chapters 4-6's safehouse sequence), we get redundant information without perspective contrast that would justify the structural choice.

**Example:** If Mara witnessed Cole's methodical preparation while internally judging his recklessness, and Cole simultaneously rationalized his approach while secretly fearing her disapproval—this would leverage dual-POV powerfully. Instead, they observe each other neutrally, which first-person doesn't justify over third-person omniscient coverage of same events.
